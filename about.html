OUR ROOTS

Born in Mbarara, Built for Uganda

Shoe Haven was founded by Software Engineering students at Mbarara University of Science and Technology (MUST) to solve a pressing community problem: finding durable, genuine, correctly sized footwear at honest prices.

We bridge the gap between reputable shoe artisans and local consumers across Western Uganda with integrated MTN & Airtel Mobile Money and fast 24-hour campus courier dispatch.
